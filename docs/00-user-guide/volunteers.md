# Volunteers: organisations, tasks and events

## Become a volunteer

There are two ways to become a volunteer:

- **Request to join:** go to **Profile → Find an Organization**. The list shows each organisation, marked *Serves your area* or *Outside your area*. Pick one, explain why you'd like to join, and tap **Submit Request**. Your profile shows *Join request pending* until an admin approves it. You are notified when that happens.
- **Invite link:** open an invite link from an organisation, sign in, and tap **Confirm & join**. The app checks that you are inside the organisation's service area.

Once you are approved, the **Tasks** and **Events** tabs appear in the app.

## Cleanup tasks

The **Tasks** tab has three views: **Assigned**, **In progress** and **Completed**. Each task card shows its priority and due date, and overdue tasks are marked.

1. Open a newly assigned task and tap **Accept** or **Decline**.
2. When you reach the site, tap **Start Task**. The task moves to *In progress*.
3. After the cleanup, tap **Add Evidence Photo** to take or choose an "after" photo. You need at least one. You can also **Add a note**.
4. Tap **Complete Task**. The organisation is notified, and the incident moves on through its workflow.

## Cleanup events

The **Events** tab lists **Upcoming** and **Past** community cleanups run by your organisation. Open an event to see its description, the linked reports and who is going. Tap **RSVP** to join or **Cancel RSVP** to leave. If the event has a limit, it shows "*n of max going*", and you can't RSVP once it is full.
